Case 01 · European pop-up

Joybuy

A European pop-up became a creator-amplified brand experience across three countries.

Zeeman shaped the activation around physical participation and digital reach, bringing creators into the experience and producing social-first content that could move across markets.

3 countries
1M+ followers reached
5M+ video views

Case 02 · Outdoor & livestream

Hazzys

An outdoor London activation connected the atmosphere of a live brand moment with audiences watching in real time.

Four London creators carried the experience across physical and digital spaces, giving the livestream a human point of view grounded in the location.

4 London creators
8K+ livestream viewers

Case 03 · Cultural craft

Zoey Filigree

A creator-led traditional craft workshop designed around intimacy, cultural participation and hands-on discovery.

Rather than treating craft as a display, the workshop invited creators and guests to take part in the process. The making itself became the story: personal, tactile and naturally suited to thoughtful content creation.

Case 04 · Cultural programme

Fluid Civilization

Exhibition, market and dialogue brought craft into a contemporary cultural conversation.

The programme created multiple ways to encounter the work: through objects, ideas and exchange. Its cultural relevance was reflected in press coverage from People’s Daily and China Minutes.
