# Zeeman Culture Website

## Purpose

Zeeman Culture is an English-first showcase website for brands seeking creator-led,
cross-cultural marketing between China and Europe. Its primary action is a project
enquiry; its secondary action is reviewing attributable case work.

## Audience

- UK and European brands reaching Chinese-speaking audiences.
- Chinese brands entering the UK and European markets.
- Prospective collaborators who need to understand Zeeman's positioning and proof.

## Approved Positioning

- A cross-cultural creative marketing agency.
- London and Shanghai perspective.
- Creator Network, Virality and Authenticity as the three homepage advantages.
- Influencer Marketing, Social Media Management and Offline Activations as the
  three service areas.

## Routes

- `/` - positioning, advantages, brand exchange, services, selected cases and contact.
- `/work/` - filterable case-study overview.
- `/services/influencer-marketing/` - influencer and creator-network proof.
- `/services/social-media-management/` - social strategy and account-development proof.
- `/services/offline-activations/` - event, launch and activation proof.
- `/about/` - company narrative, London-Shanghai model and founder profiles.
- `/contact/` - project enquiry in local test mode until Formspree is configured.
- `/privacy/` - draft privacy structure pending legal approval.

## Evidence Rules

- Attribute every metric to its named case and retain its source context.
- Link brand marks to case proof only when that proof exists on the site.
- Do not infer partnerships from reference mockups or unapproved logo lists.
- Do not publish testimonials, contact channels or performance claims without client approval.

## Brand Commitments

- Warm ivory, forest green, restrained burgundy and muted gold.
- Editorial serif headlines with clear sans-serif supporting copy.
- The approved Zeeman seal retains its distressed printed character.
- Motion supports hierarchy and remains optional under reduced-motion or Data Saver settings.

## Release Boundary

Local review comes first. A branch preview may be pushed only after local approval;
`main`, production hosting and domain settings remain unchanged until client approval.

## Current Client Asset Gaps

- Final approved brand lists and official logos for both marquee lanes.
- High-resolution case imagery and verified metric dates/sources.
- Founder profile links and approved China-facing contact details.
- Final privacy wording and future Chinese translations.

## Non-goals

- No ecommerce, authentication, CMS, analytics or cookie tracking in this version.
- No China Business Forum agenda or event-led homepage positioning.
- No invented evidence, partnership claims or generated testimonials.
