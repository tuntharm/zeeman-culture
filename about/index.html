Who we are

Culture moves through people.

Zeeman Culture is a cross-cultural creative marketing agency, with teams based in Shanghai and London. We are driven by a dual-engine model: Brands × Creators.

We provide brands with integrated marketing solutions while empowering creators to achieve sustainable growth and commercial success. We do not simply connect brands and creators; we build ecosystems that can travel across cultures.
