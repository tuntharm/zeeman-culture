# Zeeman Culture Launch Asset Checklist

The local demo uses approved pitch-deck material and temporary visual fallbacks. Replace or confirm the following before production launch.

## Required Replacements

- Four official partner logo files, preferably SVG or transparent PNG at 2x display size.
- High-resolution originals for case-study screenshots and campaign images.
- Three distinct landscape service photographs: influencer, social media and offline activation.
- Matching, professionally cropped founder portraits with approved biographies.
- Founder profile URLs for any public LinkedIn or portfolio links.
- Approved client testimonials with name, role, company and written permission.
- Dates, measurement windows and sources for every published performance metric.
- Verified China-facing email, telephone, WeChat or RED details, if these should be public.
- Company-approved privacy wording, legal entity name, registered address and retention policy.
- A Formspree form ID owned by the company.

## Current Temporary Slots

- Partner wordmarks for Sen Sen, Azuki Room, Lotus Realm and V Club are typographic placeholders.
- Existing low-resolution social screenshots are intentionally kept inside phone-sized frames.
- The homepage reuses available event photography until three distinct service images arrive.
- The privacy page is marked `noindex` and must remain a draft until legal approval.

## Future Chinese Version

- Reserve `/zh/` for approved Chinese translations.
- Translate route by route and add `hreflang` metadata only when equivalent pages exist.
- Do not expose a language switch before the translated pages are reviewed.
