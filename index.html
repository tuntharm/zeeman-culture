Who we are

Culture moves through people.

Zeeman Culture is a cross-cultural creative marketing agency, with teams based in Shanghai and London. We are driven by a dual-engine model: Brands × Creators. We provide brands with integrated marketing solutions, while empowering creators to achieve sustainable growth and commercial success. We don’t just connect brands and creators — we build ecosystems that scale across cultures.

Built in the conversations where creators, communities and brands meet.
